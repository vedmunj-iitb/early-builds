# Early builds

Three things I built at IIT Bombay between Dec 2022 and Dec 2023: a climbing line follower, an
automated chessboard and an RC flying wing.

## Climbing line follower

Course project for MS 101, IIT Bombay, Dec 2022 to Jan 2023.

A wireless line follower on an Arduino UNO that can also climb. A servo-driven shooter-and-pulley
mechanism and a 3D-printed grappling hook lifted it up to 2 m over obstacles.

## Automated chessboard

Tinkering Bootcamp, IIT Bombay, May to Dec 2023.

An electromagnet under the board moves the pieces. It is carried on a stepper-driven linear rail.
An Arduino controls the board. Reed switches in the board do the sensing.

## RC flying wing

Aeromodelling Club competition, IIT Bombay, Sep to Oct 2023.

A radio-controlled flying wing.

- a front BLDC thruster at about 25,000 RPM at full load
- two wing-mounted BLDC motors
- an ESC and a 4-channel transmitter
